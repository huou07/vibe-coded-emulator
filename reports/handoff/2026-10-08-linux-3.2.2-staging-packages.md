## Flatpak portal follow-up

- The Debian 13 builder has Flatpak 1.16.6, `xdg-desktop-portal` 1.20.3,
  `xdg-desktop-portal-gtk` 1.15.3, `fuse3`, and `/dev/fuse` installed.
- With no desktop display in the user systemd environment, the portal service
  was stuck activating and its GTK backend timed out. This matches the earlier
  failure to mount `/run/user/1000/doc`.
- With a temporary Xvfb/Openbox display on `:107` and session-only
  `DISPLAY`, `XDG_CURRENT_DESKTOP`, and `XDG_SESSION_TYPE` set, the portal
  service became active. D-Bus `Peer.Ping` succeeded, the
  `org.freedesktop.portal.FileChooser` interface exposed `OpenFile` and
  `SaveFile`, and `/run/user/1000/doc` mounted as `fuse.portal`.
- The temporary display was stopped and the systemd environment variables
  were unset. The portal service is now inactive; the document mount remains
  available in the builder's user session.
- No Flatpak app window or native picker was driven. The repository's
  `an3-control-plane` skill says the Linux desktop target needs WebKitWebDriver
  and a display and forbids native file-dialog automation. The Flatpak picker,
  import, and gameplay therefore remain **UNVERIFIED**. Direct file injection
  would not prove the picker handoff.
